The air carries the scent of sagebrush and the occasional echo of a coyote’s call, enhancing the immersive experience. The overall setting encourages slow exploration, frequent photo stops, and a steady sense of discovery, making this destination feel rewarding whether the visit is short and focused or more relaxed and unhurried.
