For ancient temples, we recommend visiting the famous Temple of Eternity, a notable attraction that offers a wealth of activities and sights, from scenic walking and photo opportunities to themed exploration and relaxed sightseeing. Visitors can explore the intricate carvings on the temple walls, admire the ancient statues of Buddhist deities, and participate in guided tours that delve into the temple’s history. The temple also features a serene garden with traditional Chinese architecture, providing a tranquil setting for meditation and reflection. With its rich historical significance, the Temple of Eternity played a crucial role in the spread of Buddhism during the Tang Dynasty, hosting important religious ceremonies and attracting pilgrims from across the region. Notably, it was visited by the renowned monk Xuanzang, who sought spiritual enlightenment and translated numerous Buddhist texts. This unique charm and historical importance make it a lively and varied destination worth recommending to different types of visitors.
